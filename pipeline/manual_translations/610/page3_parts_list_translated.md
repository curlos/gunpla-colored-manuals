## Right Page — Story & Background Text (Gundam Unicorn lore, not build instructions)

### U.C. 0096 — THE La+ MEMORY

**支配を穿つ一撃 (A Blow That Pierces Domination)**

> "Galom, below!" That stretch of debris-strewn space had become a battlefield for three mobile suits. Two Federation Forces Rezels flew in formation, chasing a single "Sleeves" machine. Two against one — a fight that should have clearly favored the Federation, but the Rezel pilots themselves knew better than anyone that it wasn't. Only moments before it had been a three-to-one advantage, and that had been whittled away with ease. The explosive flash of a downed wingman's machine revealed, floating in the void, the Sinanju — rumored to be "the return of the Red Comet." The Rezel squad leader, Major Gnome Basilicock, felt his skin crawl as past battlefield reports and his own firsthand sighting of this notorious enemy machine overlapped in his mind. *"Don't tell me this is the real Char...!?"* An overwhelming pressure that could rattle even a battle-hardened veteran like Gnome. He could see that the other Rezel pilot, Ensign Riddhe Marcenas, was completely overwhelmed — experiencing that same crushing pressure for the first time. Attacking while pinned under the enemy's dominance was no longer really attacking at all; every shot Riddhe fired was simply swallowed by the flood of debris. A one-sided battle dominated by a superior opponent, the kind Gnome had tasted before. At this rate— *"My time's about up, but Ensign Riddhe — there's something you still need to do...!"* Resolved, Gnome's Rezel charged head-on into the far stronger Sinanju!!

**宇宙と地球と (Between Space and Earth)**

> It was one-sided fighting, fit to be called an outright rout. The contradiction born when the Federation's own newly commissioned flagship, the *General Revil*, turned its murderous intent on the Federation's own *Nahel Argama*, drew in yet another contradiction: the "Sleeves" — Neo Zeon — arriving as reinforcements for the *Nahel Argama*. Their leader, Full Frontal, piloting the Sinanju, seemed to relish combat after a long absence, toying with the Federation's deployed units as he cut his way toward the flagship. Even counting the *Rosen Zulu* running interference, the Sleeves fielded only two machines — yet the *General Revil*'s tactic of deploying a massive beam-jamming barrage was, given the opponent was "the Red Comet," nothing to be ashamed of. As if he'd anticipated even that, Frontal released the dedicated bazooka mounted on his shield with one motion of his left arm, caught the arcing barrel with the rifle in his right hand, and fused the two together in an instant. What was truly astonishing was that this display — flashy enough to be mistaken for acrobatics — was, to him, just an ordinary motion for taking aim. Extending the barrel into an even larger cannon... against a Federation flagship of vastly greater scale, the fate of its crew — likely numbering well over a thousand — rested on the fingertip of a single man——.

### PSYCHO FRAME

> A structural material formed by casting, at the metal-particle level, computer chips carrying the function of a "psycommu" device — a system that receives and processes the special mental waves ("sympathetic waves") emitted by Newtypes. It doesn't just dramatically shrink psycommu equipment, which normally needs a huge amount of space — it also has various properties never seen in earlier psycommu devices, granting mobile suits significant benefits, though its full workings still aren't understood. It was adopted in U.C. 0093, during the Second Neo Zeon War, for the *Sazabi* — the personal machine of the newly risen Neo Zeon's supreme commander — but, curiously, the same technology had also been built into the opposing Federation's *ν Gundam*. The resonance between the two, believed to be one cause behind the special force field known as the "Psychofield," triggered the miraculous event that diverted the asteroid Axis from its collision course with Earth. As of U.C. 0096, manufacturing facilities exist only at the Granada factory on the Moon, kept under centralized, secrecy-focused management. The *Unicorn Gundam*, envisioned as the core of the "UC Project," adopted a Full Psychoframe skeletal structure — but several psychoframe prototype units were built beforehand to test the concept. The *Sinanju* was one of them, built specifically to test toughness and responsiveness. Separately, when the *Unicorn Gundam* activates Destroy Mode, its exposed psychoframe emits a mysterious glow — but the mechanism behind it, and why the glow color varies between individual units, are also completely unexplained phenomena.

## Notes on translation

- "スチロール樹脂：PS" is the standard Bandai material label = "Polystyrene: PS" (the plastic material used for the runner).
- "×N" after a runner name (e.g. "×2") means the kit includes **two identical copies** of that runner.
- "×印は使用しないパーツです" (top of page) = parts marked with an × symbol on the runner diagrams are **not used** in this kit and can be discarded.
- "アンダーゲート" (undergate) refers to a sprue gate located on the underside/inside of a part, designed to leave less visible nub marks after cutting — hence the special cutting instructions.
- Page 8 "PAINTING" color formulas are given as percentage mixes of named paint colors (e.g. "Monza Red," "Navy Blue") — these are generic paint-color names (matching common Gundam Marker / Mr. Color lines), not exact product codes, and Bandai's own disclaimer notes actual results may vary from the printed color swatches.
- "スミ入れ" (sumi-ire / "panel-lining") = the technique of running thinned dark paint or a fine marker into a kit's engraved panel lines to add contrast and a sense of depth.
